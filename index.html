🚦 How to use this without getting lost

  • Pick one chapter. Not the whole site. One.
  • Everything is collapsed on purpose. Click a test → it opens → the first two sections open with it. Close it when you're done and the page shrinks back down.
  • Hit Focus in the top bar to dim every card except the one you're reading.
  • Press / anywhere to jump straight into search.
  • Colours mean things. green = normal · yellow = a number to memorise · red = don't
  • Stuck on wall-of-text? Tap A+ or Aa in the top bar to make it bigger / change the font.

✅ What's normal? 🎯 Why order it? ⛔ Who can't have it? 🩺 Before / During / After 📊 What does abnormal mean?

The nursing exam almost never asks you to perform these tests. It asks what you do before (consent, NPO, allergies, baseline labs), what you watch during (sedation, vitals), and what you catch after (bleeding, perforation, respiratory depression, infection). That's the whole game.
